# Synrgy

### Connecting Talent and Opportunity with trust.

**Synrgy** is a Canadian employment-platform project focused on helping people discover legitimate work and helping employers connect with potential candidates. We're building around a simple idea: **trust should be part of the job-search experience from the start.**

🌐 **Website:** [synrgy.ca](https://synrgy.ca)  
🇨🇦 **Focus:** Canada  
🚧 **Status:** In development; the capabilities below describe our product vision, not an announcement that the service is live.

## Why Synrgy?

Searching for work should be about finding the right opportunity—not wondering whether a job advertisement or the organization behind it is genuine.

Fraudulent job offers, impersonation, and misleading postings can put candidates at risk and make it harder for legitimate employers to earn trust. Synrgy aims to address those concerns with a Canadian-focused platform built around employer verification, clear listings, and accountable moderation.

## Talent + Opportunity

We use two terms to describe the people Synrgy brings together:

- **Talent** — jobseekers and candidates exploring their next step.
- **Opportunity** — businesses and organizations looking to hire.

Both sides should have a straightforward, respectful way to connect.

## What we're building

### For Talent

- Access to job opportunities from employers who have undergone a verification process.
- Clearer information about roles, responsibilities, locations, and compensation.
- Ways to flag suspicious listings and raise concerns.
- A job-search experience designed with privacy and accessibility in mind.
- **Free access to core job searching and applications.**

### For Opportunity

- A place to reach candidates interested in genuine employment opportunities.
- Employer onboarding and verification before job postings are approved.
- Tools to create and manage job listings.
- A hiring environment where credibility and clarity matter.

### Trust as a foundation

Synrgy intends to review employer identities and job postings, respond to reports of suspicious activity, and continuously improve its safeguards. No screening system can eliminate every risk; verification is a risk-reduction measure, not a guarantee that any job, employer, or interaction is completely safe.

## Looking ahead

Our longer-term vision extends beyond a job board. We're exploring career resources and an **e-learning marketplace** where instructors could offer courses to help candidates develop skills and employers strengthen their teams.

Potential subjects include résumé writing, interview preparation, career development, recruiting, and workplace leadership. These are future possibilities rather than launched features.

## Our commitments

**Safety first.** We want sensible safeguards against fraudulent and misleading listings.

**Transparency.** We aim to explain how the platform works and avoid guarantees that cannot be supported.

**Talent-first access.** Core job searching should be free for candidates.

**Canadian focus.** We are designing Synrgy around the needs of people hiring and looking for work in Canada.

**Continuous improvement.** Feedback from candidates and employers should help shape the platform.
